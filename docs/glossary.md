# Yangon Circular Railway Glossary

## Purpose and authority

This is the canonical vocabulary for YCR product, domain, API, data, and documentation work. Use the English terms in the **Canonical English** column exactly, including the code identifier where one is shown.

The requested source documents do not provide authoritative Myanmar-script translations. Every missing Myanmar term is therefore recorded as **OPEN QUESTION - authoritative term not supplied**. No agent may invent a Myanmar translation. A proposed translation must be supplied by the appropriate Myanma Railways language or business owner and then reviewed into this glossary.

Accepted ADRs are binding. Superseded ADRs are retained as historical context only; their terms do not override the current ADR listed in the source column. Business-dependent meanings remain OPEN QUESTION where the source says so.

The **Forbidden synonyms** column applies when referring to the exact canonical concept in that row. A listed word may still be used for a genuinely different concept.

## Actor and role labels

These are canonical labels for the actors named in **03**, with the **role identifier** F-002 implements for each. The eight identifiers are a **BUSINESS DECISION - provisional tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-23; OQ12, F-002 spec R19) - not a Myanma Railways answer**: OQ12 stays open for an official answer, which would supersede them. A user may hold several roles; roles are not station-scoped. The role-to-permission grants are **10**'s: `stations.*` resolved under OQ28 and the identity permissions ruled for F-002; every other grant is still an OPEN QUESTION there. Outside those two uses the canonical label is required - in prose, code, identifiers, API documentation, permission names and audit actions.

A route has no direction. On a closed route a service may **wrap** (from the last station to the first going `Forward`, from the first to the last going `Reverse`) and may cover at most one circuit; a **full circuit** returns to its first station as its last stop, covering exactly one circuit with at least 3 distinct stations. A station appears at most once among a service's stops, except a full circuit's closing stop. A service has at least 2 stops and never changes them. | Effective period (`EffectiveFrom` / `EffectiveTo`) | OPEN QUESTION - authoritative term not supplied | The calendar dates over which a service applies (Asia/Yangon dates, `DateOnly`/`date`): `EffectiveFrom` is required and is the first date; `EffectiveTo` is the last date, **inclusive**, or null for **open-ended**. At creation `EffectiveTo` is not earlier than `EffectiveFrom` nor than today; `EffectiveFrom` may be in the past. Only a withdrawal changes it. It is not a `ScheduleVersion`'s effective date (FR-004). Provisional tech-lead rulings, not a Myanma Railways answer (OQ48, OQ50, hein 2026-09-25, T-044). **07**, ADR-0018, `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R19, R35, R39, `src/YCR.Domain/Timetable/EffectivePeriod.cs`. | Validity window (a ticket concept), timetable version, active period, EffectiveUntil |
| Withdrawal | OPEN QUESTION - authoritative term not supplied | The only change a service allows: withdrawing it **from a date D**, the first date on which it no longer runs, sets `EffectiveTo` to D − 1 day and records `WithdrawnAtUtc` (the latest withdrawal's instant). D is not earlier than today (Asia/Yangon), and a withdrawal must shorten the period; a later withdrawal may shorten it further. A service withdrawn so that `EffectiveTo` is earlier than `EffectiveFrom` **never runs** (`neverRuns`), overlaps nothing, and keeps its row. A change of stopping pattern is a withdrawal from D plus a new service with the same code from D; a change of times is a new timetable version (OQ54). **Since F-005, a withdrawal from D is refused** (`422 Timetable.ServiceInPublishedScheduleVersion`) while a published timetable version that lists the service applies on any date on or after D; to drop a listed service, publish a version without it, then withdraw the service from that version's start date. Withdrawal is not deletion or deactivation, and a timetable version is cancelled, not withdrawn. Provisional tech-lead rulings, not a Myanma Railways answer (OQ48, hein 2026-09-25, T-044; OQ54, hein 2026-09-26, T-053). **07**, `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R20, R21, R36, R41, R42, `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` R19, R20. | Delete, cancel, deactivate, suspend, retire |
| ScheduleVersion | OPEN QUESTION - authoritative term not supplied | A versioned timetable aggregate that can be published and then treated as immutable (row of `timetable.ScheduleVersions`, API `/schedules/versions`). It has a version number, a `BilingualName` and a start date `EffectiveFrom` (no end date). It is created whole as a draft and never edited; only its status changes. Provisional tech-lead rulings, not a Myanma Railways answer (OQ51, OQ56, hein 2026-09-26, T-053). ## Maintenance rules

1. Use the canonical English term in code, API documentation, database documentation, and tests.
2. Do not introduce a new synonym to avoid an unresolved business question. Add an OPEN QUESTION to `docs/19-open-questions.md` instead.
3. Do not use a role name as a permission, or a permission as a role. OQ12 and OQ28 govern role grants.
4. Keep `Ticket`, `Sale`, `Payment`, `Refund`, and `TicketValidation` as independent concepts. ADR-0013 prohibits collapsing their lifecycles.
5. Treat `Expired` and `Used` as derived conditions where the ADRs say so; do not add them as stored Ticket states without a superseding ADR. Both have rows above.
6. When an authoritative Myanmar term is approved, update the Myanmar term column and cite the approving source; do not silently replace `OPEN QUESTION` entries.
7. Keep coverage of **04** complete: every candidate aggregate, value object and domain service listed in **04** has a row here. When **04** gains or renames one, change this file in the same commit.
8. A row marked **Provisional - owned by T-014** carries a value unblocked only by a provisional tech-lead assumption, not by a Myanma Railways decision. Do not present it as settled, and do not remove the marker outside T-014.
9. When a source document and an Accepted ADR disagree on a name, the ADR wins, and the row records that the conflict existed and how it was resolved. Changing the ADR instead requires a superseding ADR (AGENTS.md, Binding decisions).
