29. ~~How must the system treat Zawgyi-encoded Myanmar text on input?~~ Zawgyi text occupies the same Unicode code points as standard Myanmar, so storing names as `nvarchar` satisfies `docs/20` §6's "never store Zawgyi" only if Zawgyi never arrives.     **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-22; T-014) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** Option (c): no server-side detector is built; producing Unicode input is a client requirement. F-001's existing validation (presence and length only) already satisfies this, so no code change was needed. If Myanma Railways or a later usability finding requires a server-side control, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

34. How are staff accounts governed? Specifically: who may create a staff account, disable it, assign or remove roles and reset its password; whether segregation of duties applies (may an administrator grant a role to themselves, and does granting a privileged role need a second approver); what identifies a staff member in the system (username format, a Myanma Railways employee number, a name in Myanmar script); and which person or office holds the first administrator account. **BLOCKS:** F-002's account-administration API and its bootstrap of the first administrator (`docs/features/F-002-staff-authentication/spec.md` D9, D10, §6.2). Related to OQ12 (which roles exist) and OQ28 (role grants). (Raised by T-023, 2026-09-23.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-23; T-023) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one, which unblocks F-002's account administration and bootstrap: `SystemAdministrator` creates and disables accounts, assigns roles and resets passwords; no user may change their own roles; no second approver in Phase 1; a staff member is identified by a username of 3–50 characters from lowercase `a`–`z`, `0`–`9` and `.`; hein holds the first administrator account until Myanma Railways names a holder. **Added 2026-09-23 (hein; T-023, U5):** no administrator may disable or administrator-reset their own account (they use their own password change instead), and at least one active `SystemAdministrator` must always remain — the system refuses disabling the last one or removing its role (`422 Identity.LastAdministrator`; spec R20, R27). **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-002-staff-authentication/spec.md` R20, D9, D10.

36. Which routes does the Yangon Circular Railway have, for this system's purposes? Specifically: is the whole network one closed loop defined once; two routes, one per direction of travel; or does it also include short workings, spurs, branches or other lines that share YCR stations? And who is the authority for the route list? `docs/01` FR-002 says "railway lines/routes" without defining either term, and no document names a single route. **BLOCKS:** F-003 route management — the Route aggregate's shape, whether a route carries a direction, and any route master data (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R5, §6, §7). Related to OQ1 (station list) and OQ17 (loop fare direction). (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: the system may hold several routes, and routes may share stations. A route has **no direction attribute**; direction belongs to services (FR-003) and fares (OQ17). There is **no separate `Line` concept**: "line" in FR-002 is read as "route" (this closes F-003 contradiction C1; the glossary's forbidden-synonym rule stands). The YCR loop is entered as one route; no seed data ships (OQ1). **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R5.

37. What does a route's ordered station sequence look like? Specifically: is a sequence closed (the last station connects back to the first, as on a loop) or open (it has two ends); may the same station appear more than once in one sequence (for example the start station repeated to close the loop, or a route that passes a station twice); and what is the minimum number of stations in a route? **BLOCKS:** F-003's sequence validation and its database constraints — whether `(RouteId, StationId)` is unique, and whether a route stores a closed/open attribute (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R6, R7, R8). Related to OQ36. (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: each route has a yes/no closed-or-open setting, stored as a column (`IsClosed bit`). A closed route runs from its last station back to its first; that connection comes from the setting, and the first station is never repeated at the end. A station never appears twice in one sequence, so the unique index on `(RouteId, StationId)` applies. Minimum length: 2 stations for an open route, 3 for a closed one. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R6–R8.

38. Can a route's station sequence change after it is first defined, and if it can, must earlier sequences be kept, with effective dates, in the way `docs/07` §Rules requires for fare and schedule versions? Or is a route mutable master data whose current sequence simply replaces the previous one? Which later features must be able to reconstruct the sequence that applied on a past date — fares (ADR-0002: "historical tickets must remain explainable"), timetables, ticketing, reporting? **BLOCKS:** F-003's sequence-change operation, and whether its tables hold versions and are insert-only (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R9, R10, §6, §7). (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: option (c), **immutable**. A route's sequence can never change after creation; to change the network, an administrator creates a new route and deactivates the old one. There is no `PUT /routes/{id}/stations` and no sequence-replacement operation, and `RouteStations` is insert-only (`ycr_app` has `SELECT`, `INSERT` only). Because stored sequences never change, they are their own history. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R9, R10.

39. How do routes and inactive stations interact? Specifically: may an inactive station be placed in a route's sequence; and when a station that is in a route is deactivated (F-001 `DeactivateStation`), is the deactivation refused, allowed with the station staying in the sequence, or allowed with the station removed from the sequence? **BLOCKS:** F-003's sequence validation, and any change to F-001's station deactivation (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R11, R12, S16–S18). Related to OQ38: removing a station from a sequence is a sequence change. (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: an inactive station cannot be placed in a route (`422 Network.RouteStationInactive`). Deactivating a station that is in a route is allowed, and the station stays in the sequence; route reads show it with `isActive = false`. F-001's `DeactivateStation` does **not** change. Accepted consequence: a station deactivated by mistake cannot be placed in a new route until station reactivation ships (accepted for now; no task created). **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R11, R12.

41. What identifies a route, and what is its lifecycle? Specifically: does a route have an official code and names (English and Myanmar), what are their formats and uniqueness rules, may a code be reused; and can a route be withdrawn — deactivated and reactivated, or deleted? **BLOCKS:** F-003's route fields, their validation and unique constraints, and any route deactivation operation (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R13, R14, R15). Related to OQ26 and OQ27, which settled the same questions for stations. (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: a route has a code and a `BilingualName`, under exactly the station rules (F-001 R3/R4; OQ26/OQ27): code 2–10 characters of `A`–`Z` and `0`–`9`, unique across all routes including inactive ones, never reused; `NameEn` and `NameMy` required, each 1–100 characters after trimming, not unique. A route can be deactivated only (`POST /routes/{id}/deactivate`; again → `422 Network.RouteAlreadyInactive`), and the row is kept. No reactivation, no delete, and no `PATCH /routes/{id}`: code and names are fixed after creation, because of the immutable model (OQ38) and the F-001 §9 rename-history question. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R13–R15.
