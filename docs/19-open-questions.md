37. What does a route's ordered station sequence look like? Specifically: is a sequence closed (the last station connects back to the first, as on a loop) or open (it has two ends); may the same station appear more than once in one sequence (for example the start station repeated to close the loop, or a route that passes a station twice); and what is the minimum number of stations in a route? **BLOCKS:** F-003's sequence validation and its database constraints — whether `(RouteId, StationId)` is unique, and whether a route stores a closed/open attribute (`docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` R6, R7, R8). Related to OQ36. (Raised by T-032, 2026-09-24.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: each route has a yes/no closed-or-open setting, stored as a column (`IsClosed bit`). A closed route runs from its last station back to its first; that connection comes from the setting, and the first station is never repeated at the end. A station never appears twice in one sequence, so the unique index on `(RouteId, StationId)` applies. Minimum length: 2 stations for an open route, 3 for a closed one. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-003-route-management/spec.md` §0.8, R6–R8.

38.     **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-24; T-032) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: option (c), **immutable**. 44. How does a service express its direction and its extent on a route? The OQ36 ruling put direction on services, and a route has no direction. On a closed route: does a service run clockwise or anticlockwise relative to the route's station order; where does it start and end; may it run a full circuit and end at the station it started from; may it run more than one circuit? On an open route: may a service run in either direction, and may it run only part of the route? **BLOCKS:** F-004's direction attribute, start and end stations, and the order check on a stopping pattern (`docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R9, R10). Related to OQ36, OQ37 and OQ17. (Raised by T-044, 2026-09-25.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: A service stores `Direction` = `Forward` or `Reverse`, relative to its route's station order, and its stops follow the route order in that direction. On a closed route the stops may wrap from the route's last station to its first (`Forward`) or from its first to its last (`Reverse`). A full circuit is expressed by repeating the first stop as the last stop: only on a closed route, only as the final stop, and at most one circuit. Running over part of a route is allowed on open and closed routes, and reverse running on an open route is allowed. See `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` §0.9, R9, R10, R12. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: Every stop is a station of the service's route, and a service has exactly one route. Specifically: is it a set of days of the week; is there a public-holiday calendar, and if so who maintains it and which holidays it holds (including lunar-dated Myanmar holidays); are there per-date exceptions — extra running days and cancelled days; and is a service's operating day the calendar date on which it leaves its first stop? **BLOCKS:** F-004's operating-day model and its storage (`docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R17, R18, §7). (Raised by T-044, 2026-09-25.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: **Partly resolved.** Operating days are a set of days of the week only, with at least one day. The operating date is the Asia/Yangon calendar date on which the service starts; this becomes meaningful only once FR-004 adds times. **A public-holiday calendar and per-date exceptions (extra or cancelled running days) remain OPEN with Myanma Railways**; F-004 records them as a known limitation and implements no placeholder. See `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` §0.9, R17, R18, §9. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

48. What is a service's effective period, and can a service change after it is defined? Specifically: is a start date required and may the end date be open; can the stopping pattern, operating days or effective period change later, and if so must the earlier definition be kept (`docs/07` §Rules "Preserve historical fare/schedule versions"; `docs/08` "published … timetable versions are immutable"); can a service be withdrawn early, reactivated or deleted; may two definitions of the same service overlap in time; and how does a service's effective period relate to the effective dates of a timetable version (FR-004)? **BLOCKS:** F-004 — whether a service is immutable, versioned or edited in place, `PATCH /services`, the database grants, and whether ADR-0024 applies (`docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R19–R21, §5, §6, §7). Related to OQ38, which chose "immutable" for routes, and to OQ4. (Raised by T-044, 2026-09-25.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: A service is immutable except for withdrawal. `EffectiveFrom` is required; `EffectiveTo` is inclusive and may be null (open-ended). Withdrawal takes a date D and sets `EffectiveTo` = D − 1 day; it may only shorten the period (never extend it, never reopen it), and D must not be earlier than today's Asia/Yangon date. If D ≤ `EffectiveFrom` the service never runs. There is no other edit, no reactivation and no delete. A timetable change is: withdraw the old service from D, and create a new one with the same code from D. See `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` §0.9, R19–R21, R35, R36. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

49. Which roles may manage and read services, trains (if OQ42 keeps them) and timetable versions? `docs/10-authorization-matrix.md` lists `trains.manage`, `services.manage` and `schedules.manage` with role grants as OPEN QUESTION, has no row for them in its proposal table, and has no read permission for any of them (the gap OQ28 and OQ40 had for stations and routes). **BLOCKS:** F-004's endpoint permissions and its grant seed migration (`docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R3, R4, §2). Related to OQ12, OQ28 and OQ40. (Raised by T-044, 2026-09-25.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: `services.manage` → `SystemAdministrator` and `RailwayAdministrator`; `services.read` (permission name added by hein, 2026-09-25, E6) → all eight roles: `SystemAdministrator`, `RailwayAdministrator`, `StationManager`, `TicketOperator`, `TicketInspector`, `FinanceOfficer`, `Auditor`, `ReportingUser`. No `trains.*` grants (OQ42). `schedules.*` is deferred to FR-004 and unchanged in the inventory. Recorded as the approved grants in `docs/10-authorization-matrix.md` §Service permission grants. See `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` §0.9, R3, R4. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

50. May a service be created with an effective period that starts, or ends, before today's Asia/Yangon date? The OQ48 ruling bars a withdrawal date earlier than today, so a withdrawal never rewrites the past, but it says nothing about creation. A past `EffectiveFrom` would record that a service ran on dates that are already over — for example when the timetable in force at go-live is first entered, or when a forgotten service is recorded late. Is a past `EffectiveFrom` (a) refused, (b) allowed, or (c) allowed only for an initial load? And may `EffectiveTo`, if given at creation, be earlier than today? **BLOCKS:** F-004's effective-period validation at creation (`docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` R39, S40). Related to OQ48 and OQ1. (Raised by T-044, 2026-09-25, while applying the OQ48 ruling.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-25; T-044) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: a service may be created with `EffectiveFrom` earlier than today's Asia/Yangon date. `EffectiveTo`, if given, must not be earlier than today (`422 Timetable.ServiceEffectiveToInPast`). The overlap rule for a code applies unchanged. `CreatedAtUtc` and the `Timetable.ServiceCreated` audit event record when the service was entered. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task. See `docs/features/F-004-service-management/spec.md` §0.10, R39, S40, S40a.

51. What is a timetable version (`ScheduleVersion`), and what does one contain? Specifically: does one version cover **every service on the network**, the services of **one route**, or **one service**; is a service in at most one version at a time, or may several versions list it; what identifies a version (a number, a code, a name in English and Myanmar, a label such as "October 2026 timetable") and what are its formats and uniqueness rules; and which effective dates does it carry — a start date only (the version stays in force until another replaces it) or a start and an end? `docs/01` FR-004 says only "timetable versions with effective dates"; `docs/04` lists `ScheduleVersion` as a candidate aggregate "to confirm through discovery". **BLOCKS:** F-005's `ScheduleVersion` aggregate and tables, its identity fields and their constraints, and every other F-005 rule (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G1, R6–R9, §6, §7). Related to OQ48 and OQ55. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: one version covers **the whole network**. It is identified by a system-assigned sequential **number** (assigned at creation, never reused) and a `BilingualName` under the shared name rules (`NameEn` and `NameMy` required, 1–100 characters after trimming, not unique). It has a **start date only** (`EffectiveFrom`, `DateOnly`) and is in force until the next published version's start date (OQ55). See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R6–R8. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

52. What times does a timetable give for a service's stops? Specifically: an arrival time, a departure time, or both, at each stop — and at the first stop (departure only?) and the last stop (arrival only?); the precision (whole minutes, or seconds); whether a minimum or maximum dwell time (departure − arrival at one stop) applies; whether times must strictly increase from stop to stop; whether the timetable also gives **passing times** at stations the service runs through without stopping (F-004 allows passing stations, OQ45 ruling); and whether a service's times may differ by day of the week, or are the same on every operating day. **BLOCKS:** F-005's stop-time fields, their validation and storage (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G2, R10–R14, §7). Related to OQ45 and OQ47. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: the first stop has a **departure** only, the last stop an **arrival** only, and every intermediate stop both; times are in **whole minutes**; at each stop arrival ≤ departure; each stop's arrival is strictly later than the previous stop's departure; there are **no passing times** for stations the service does not stop at; a service has **the same times on every operating day**. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R10–R14. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

53. May a service run past midnight? The OQ47 ruling makes a service's operating date the Asia/Yangon calendar date on which it **starts**. If a service may run past midnight: how is a time after midnight written in the timetable (for example `00:15` on the next day, or `24:15`); is there a maximum journey length (under 24 hours?); and does the next day's weekday matter for anything, such as the operating days (OQ47) or a public holiday? If a service may not run past midnight, every service starts and ends on one calendar date. **BLOCKS:** F-005's time range and past-midnight handling (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G3, R15, R16; ADR-0027, Proposed). Related to OQ47 and OQ52. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: **no running past midnight in Phase 1.** Every time is 00:00–23:59 on the operating date, and the whole journey falls within one operating date (a known limitation). ADR-0027 is Accepted with a maximum of 1439 minutes. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R15, R16. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

54. How do a timetable version's effective dates relate to the effective periods of the services in it? `docs/01` FR-003 gives *services* effective periods and FR-004 gives *versions* effective dates, and F-004 left the relationship to FR-004 (F-004 spec C5, closure note). Specifically: must every service in a version be effective on every date of the version, or only on some of them, or is a service's period independent (the service runs on a date only when both the service's period and the version cover it)? May a version list a service that has been withdrawn (OQ48) or never runs? When a service in a **published** version is later withdrawn from date D (F-004 withdrawal), is the withdrawal refused, allowed with the version unchanged (the service simply stops running from D), or does it require a new version? And the OQ48 ruling says a timetable change is "withdraw the old service from D and create a new one with the same code from D" — is that still how a timetable changes, or does a new version replace it, or are both used? **BLOCKS:** F-005's version-content validation and its effect on F-004's `WithdrawService` (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G4, C1, C2, R17–R20). Related to OQ48, OQ50 and OQ51. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: a service runs on a date only if the version in force on that date lists it, the date is in the service's own effective period and its weekday is an operating day. Every service a version lists must be effective, and not withdrawn, on the version's start date. **Withdrawing a service from date D (F-004) is refused** (`422 Timetable.ServiceInPublishedScheduleVersion`) while any published, not-cancelled version that lists it applies on any date ≥ D; a version applies from its start date to the day before the next published, not-cancelled version's start date, or open-ended. Dropping a service is therefore: publish a version without it, then withdraw the service from that version's start date. Timetable changes use both mechanisms: services for stopping patterns, versions for times. This closes F-005 contradictions C1 and C2, and **amends the OQ48 withdrawal rule** with the refusal above. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R17–R20. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.
    **Q2 — provisional tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053, at F-005 approval) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** **A withdrawal stays in force when a later cancellation revives an earlier version.** If a service was withdrawn from D (allowed because the version starting on D does not list it) and that version is then cancelled (OQ58 ruling), the cancel succeeds and is not refused; the service stays withdrawn. The earlier version applies again from D, still lists the service, and reports it as not running from D (`runsOnDate = false`). Likewise a version may be published whose range includes dates after a listed service's end. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10 Q2, R49, SV41, SV54. **Still open with Myanma Railways**, as above.

55. How do timetable versions follow one another? Specifically: may more than one version be in force on the same date (within the scope OQ51 sets); does a newly published version end the one before it automatically from its own start date, or must each version carry its own end date; may versions overlap; may there be a gap — dates on which no version is in force, and what happens to services on those dates; may a version be published to take effect **before** an already-published future version (inserted between two versions); and must every earlier version be kept and remain readable after it is replaced (`docs/07` §Rules: "Preserve historical fare/schedule versions")? **BLOCKS:** F-005's supersession rule, any "version in force on date D" read, and whether publication needs a set-invariant lock (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G5, R21–R25; ADR-0026). Related to OQ51 and OQ54. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: **exactly one version is in force on each date**: the published, not-cancelled version with the latest start date on or before it. Start dates are unique among published, not-cancelled versions (a filtered unique index). Before the first version, no service runs. A version may be published with a start date between two future ones. Every version is kept (history). See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R21–R25. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

56. How is a timetable version prepared before it is published? Specifically: is a draft built up and changed over time (services added, removed, times corrected) before publication, or is it created complete in one step and, if wrong, discarded and created again; may a draft be deleted or discarded, and must a discarded draft be kept; and may several drafts exist at the same time? `docs/20` §4 and `docs/08` fix only that a version is created, then published, and never changed once published. **BLOCKS:** F-005's draft operations and whether ADR-0024 (Proposed, including its child-row note) must be Accepted first (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G6, R26–R29, §5, §6). Related to OQ38 and OQ48, which chose "immutable" for routes and services. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: a draft is **created whole** (all services, with all their times, in one request) and never edited; a wrong draft is **discarded** (status `Discarded`, row kept, never deleted); several drafts may exist at once. ADR-0024 is not used and stays Proposed. The lifecycle is Draft → Published → (Cancelled), or Draft → Discarded. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R26–R29. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

57. Who publishes a timetable version, and under what conditions? Specifically: may the person who prepared a version also publish it, or must a second person approve it (maker-checker, as ADR-0013 assumes for refunds); is publication a separate right from preparing drafts; how far in advance must a version be published before it takes effect — may it take effect today, and may it be published with a start date **in the past** (compare OQ50 for services, which allowed a past `EffectiveFrom`); and must a version be published before any service in it may run (or sell tickets, once OQ4 is answered)? **BLOCKS:** F-005's publish operation, its guards and its permission split (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G7, R30–R33). Related to OQ34 (no second approver for staff accounts in Phase 1), OQ50 and OQ58. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: any holder of `schedules.manage` may publish, including the draft's author; there is **no second approver** in Phase 1. The start date is set when the draft is created and must be on or after today's Asia/Yangon date at publish time; a draft whose start date has passed can no longer be published and can only be discarded. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R30, R31. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.

58. Can a published timetable version be withdrawn or cancelled? `docs/08` and `docs/20` §4 make a published version immutable (no `PATCH`), but say nothing about withdrawing one. Specifically: may a published version that has **not yet taken effect** be cancelled; may a version **in force** be withdrawn from a date D, and if so, which version is in force from D (the previous one again, or none); and is a correction to a published version only ever made by publishing a new one? **BLOCKS:** any F-005 withdraw or cancel operation on a published version (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.2 G7, R34, §5). Related to OQ48 (service withdrawal) and OQ55. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: a published version may be **cancelled only while its start date is later than today** (Asia/Yangon); after that it is corrected by publishing a later version. Cancelling is a status change (Published → Cancelled); a cancelled version is kept and never applies. Withdrawing a version that has already taken effect is not provided. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R34. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.
    **Q2 — provisional tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053, at F-005 approval) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** **Cancelling a version is not refused because of an earlier service withdrawal.** If a service was withdrawn from D (allowed because the version starting on D does not list it) and that version is then cancelled, the cancel succeeds; the earlier version applies again from D and still lists the service, which stays withdrawn and is reported as not running from D (`runsOnDate = false`). See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10 Q2, R49, SV41, SV54, and the Q2 note under OQ54. **Still open with Myanma Railways**, as above.

59. Which roles may prepare, publish and read timetable versions? `docs/10-authorization-matrix.md` lists `schedules.manage` with role grants OPEN QUESTION (the OQ49 ruling deferred it to FR-004), has no read permission for timetable versions, and has no row for them in its proposal table (the gap OQ28, OQ40 and OQ49 closed for stations, routes and services). 60. May a timetable version list **no services**? The OQ51 ruling makes a version cover the whole network and the OQ55 ruling makes exactly one version in force on each date, but neither says whether a version may be empty. An empty published version would mean that no service runs from its start date until the next version — for example a network-wide suspension. Options (non-binding): (a) refused, at least one service; (b) allowed. **BLOCKS:** F-005's request validation for `POST /schedules/versions` (`docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.11, R9, SV22). Related to OQ51 and OQ55. (Raised by T-053, 2026-09-26, while applying the OQ51–OQ59 rulings.)
    **Resolved by tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-26; T-053) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** This project chose not to wait for one: (b) a version **may list no services**. Publishing an empty version means no service runs from its start date until the next published version's start date (a network-wide suspension). It is corrected like any other version: cancelled while its start date is later than today (OQ58 ruling), or superseded by a later published version. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10, R9, SV22, SV53. **Still open with Myanma Railways.** If Myanma Railways later gives an official, different answer, that supersedes this ruling and needs its own follow-up task.
    **Amendment 1 — provisional tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-27; T-054) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** A version that lists no services may only be **published with a start date later than today** (Asia/Yangon); publishing one on its start date is refused (`422 Timetable.EmptyScheduleVersionNotInFuture`) and nothing is written. A network-wide suspension can therefore always be cancelled before it takes effect. Creating an empty draft is unchanged. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10 Amendment 1, R50, SV55, SV56. **Still open with Myanma Railways**, as above.
    **Amendment 2 — provisional tech-lead ruling (hein, 2026-09-27; T-054 Q3) — not a Myanma Railways answer.** An empty draft whose start date is not later than today is **refused at creation too**, with the same `422 Timetable.EmptyScheduleVersionNotInFuture`, and nothing is written (such a draft could never be published). Amendment 1's "Creating an empty draft is unchanged" is replaced by this. The publication check stays, for a draft created with a later start date that has since become today. See `docs/features/F-005-timetables/spec.md` §0.10 Amendment 2, R50, SV55. **Still open with Myanma Railways**, as above.
