### What the glossary gets right

Recorded so a remediator does not undo it:

- **The Myanmar-term discipline is exactly right, and it holds without exception.** All 102 rows carry `OPEN QUESTION - authoritative term not supplied`, and the file has **zero non-ASCII bytes** — there is no half-invented term, no transliteration, no "TBD" that a later reader might mistake for an answer. §Purpose states plainly that no agent may invent one and that a proposal must come from a Myanma Railways language or business owner, and maintenance rule 6 closes the loop by requiring the approving source to be cited when a term does arrive. This was the hardest thing to get right in this task and it is not merely passed but well built.
- **Provisional values are marked as provisional.** The `StationCode` row says the format "remains an open business question (OQ26)" rather than presenting F-001's placeholder as settled. `PassengerCategory` (OQ9), `PrintSequence` (OQ23), `Validity window` (OQ19), `AuthenticUnverified` (OQ8), `Cancellation` (OQ10) and `Rounding policy` (OQ21) all name their blocking question.
- **The actor section refuses to imply a role model.** Line 61 states that including the labels "does not approve the role model or any role-to-permission grant", and maintenance rule 3 forbids using a role as a permission. Given that OQ12 and OQ28 are open, this is the correct posture and it is stated twice.
- **Both cited permissions are real.** `stations.manage` and `tickets.validate` both trace to `docs/10`'s approved inventory. Nothing was coined.
- **The line-11 carve-out is genuinely load-bearing and mostly well judged.** It is what correctly permits `StationId` for the GUID, "repository" for the git repo, and "cashier session" for `CashierSession`. Without it the forbidden lists would be unusable; G-4 and G-6 are about extending the same judgement two places further, not about reversing it.
- **`docs/04`'s aggregates and value objects are fully covered** — 10 / 10 and 8 / 8.

## Verdict

**Ready, with findings. No blocking finding, and nothing needs to be undone.**

No Critical or High. The property that matters most in this task — **that no Myanmar term was invented** — holds completely and verifiably, and the surrounding discipline (provisional values marked, no role grants implied, no permission coined) holds with it.

Three Medium findings, one of which is not the author's to resolve:

- **G-1 needs a tech-lead ruling.** The glossary canonicalises `TicketValidationRecord` while binding **ADR-0013 says `TicketValidation`**, and cites the ADR as a concurring source. Someone has to decide which name wins; if the ADR is the one that changes, AGENTS.md §Binding decisions requires a superseding ADR rather than a doc edit.
- **G-2** marks `PrintCount` — a real, separate field that ADR-0013 requires reprinting to increment — as a forbidden synonym of `PrintSequence`. Applied faithfully, the glossary would delete a binding concept.
- **G-3** omits `BilingualName` / `NameEn` / `NameMy`, the one domain type that stores Myanmar script, from the glossary whose defining caveat is the absence of authoritative Myanmar terms.

Six Low findings (G-4 to G-9) are coverage and internal-consistency hygiene: two `docs/04` domain services missing, `docs/05` and `docs/06` forbidden-synonym hits needing the same carve-out `docs/10` already got, two self-uses of forbidden synonyms in the glossary's own prose, a `Used` row that rule 5 implies but does not exist, and the `AuditEvent` identifier missing from the audit row.

### What this verdict does not cover

- **Whether the canonical English terms are the *right* choices** where no source dictates one. I checked them against `docs/03`–`11`, the ADRs and the F-001 code at `9106d53`, and report where they conflict. Whether "Fare quote" beats "Quote" as a name, absent a source that settles it, is a naming preference, not a review finding.
- **The forbidden-synonym lists' completeness.** I checked that the listed synonyms do not appear where they shouldn't; I did not try to establish that every synonym worth forbidding is listed.
- **Documents outside `docs/03`–`11`, the ADRs and F-001.** `docs/12`, `docs/17` and `docs/18` are cited by individual glossary rows and I read the cited passages, but I did not sweep them for forbidden synonyms.
