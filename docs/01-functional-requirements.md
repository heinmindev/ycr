# Functional Requirements

## FR-001 Station management

Administrators can create, update, activate/deactivate, search and view stations.

## FR-002 Route management

Administrators can define railway lines/routes and ordered station sequences.

## FR-003 Service management

Authorized users can define train services, operating days, effective periods and stopping patterns.

## FR-004 Timetables

Authorized users can publish timetable versions with effective dates.

## FR-005 Fare management

Authorized users can create versioned fare rules.

## FR-006 Ticket purchase

The system calculates a fare, creates a ticket, records sale/payment state and returns a ticket identifier.

## FR-007 Ticket validation

A validator/operator can verify authenticity, status, validity period and duplicate-use conditions.

## FR-008 Cancellation

Authorized users can cancel an eligible ticket according to the active cancellation policy.

## FR-009 Refund

The system creates at most one refund for a refundable financial transaction unless an explicitly supported partial-refund workflow exists.

## FR-010 Audit

Security-sensitive and financially significant operations must be auditable.

## FR-011 Reporting

Provide sales, validation, cancellation, refund and reconciliation reports.
